The company

African science for living soils and resilient farms.

MarinBio Agritech Solutions Ltd is a Kenyan biotechnology, circular bioeconomy and climate-smart agriculture company developing biological products, waste-valorization systems, controlled-environment agriculture and digital support tools.

Mission

Transform agricultural waste into high-performance biological technologies that regenerate soils, improve productivity and strengthen food security.

Vision

Become Africa’s leading biological technology company powering regenerative agriculture through biotechnology, circular bioeconomy, digital agriculture and AI.
